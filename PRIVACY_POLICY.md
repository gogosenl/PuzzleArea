# Puzzle Area Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Puzzle Area is a mobile puzzle game published by Görsen Games. This document summarizes the services integrated into the application. Store-facing privacy disclosures must always be kept consistent with the versions distributed through Apple App Store and Google Play.

## Local Game Data

Puzzle Area does not require an account. Progress and preferences are stored locally on the device, including completed and unlocked levels, earned stars, in-game currency balances, settings, and onboarding status.

## Advertising

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads for interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising data, diagnostics, and interaction information according to their own policies and the consent choices available to the user. Players who purchase the permanent remove-ads product do not receive interstitial or rewarded ads after the entitlement is restored.

For provider-specific details, see [Google Mobile Ads data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## In-App Purchases

Optional digital products are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Puzzle Area receives purchase status and product information needed to deliver and restore the purchased content. Payment-card details are handled by the relevant store and are not received by Puzzle Area.

See [Apple In-App Purchase](https://developer.apple.com/in-app-purchase/) and [Google Play Payments](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738) for store-specific information.

## Diagnostics

The app may retain a limited technical error history locally on the device to help diagnose crashes. This local log is not automatically uploaded to a developer-operated server.

## Permissions

Puzzle Area does not request access to the microphone, contacts, location, or photo library for its core gameplay.

## Children

Puzzle Area does not knowingly request names, email addresses, phone numbers, or similar directly identifying information from children.

## Data Removal

Local game data can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing its application data. Store purchase records remain subject to Apple or Google account and retention policies.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the Puzzle Area store listing.
